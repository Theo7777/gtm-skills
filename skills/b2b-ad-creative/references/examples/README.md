# Reference ads

Ads for fictional brands, made as references for this skill. Open the folder for a format before writing its copy. Take the layout ideas, never the words.

These are image-model renders, so some effects (3D phones, glass shields, painted light) cannot be rebuilt from editable Figma shapes. Match their hierarchy, colour discipline, and finish, not those effects.

| Folder | Ads | What to take from them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `before-after/` | Relay, Kairova | Two equal panels: a grey, messy "before" (sticky notes, a pile of reply-all emails, crossed-out tasks with no owner) against a brand-colour "after" holding one calm product screen |
| `product-in-action/` | Metric, Nivaro | Big outcome headline, a CTA pill, then one real-looking screen bleeding off an edge, on a flat pale brand tint |
| `testimonial/` | Pennywork, Bravio | Solid dark brand colour, large quote marks, the payoff phrase in the accent colour, small attribution. Sans and logo-only (Pennywork) or serif with a round headshot (Bravio) |
| `value-prop-logos/` | Forma, Havelo | One accent phrase, a CTA, then a label and five or six logos in one tone and one size. Light (Forma) or dark (Havelo) |
| `icp-background/` | Vaultline, Avenlo | A full-bleed photo of the buyer at work, the headline in the calm space, pills or one product card on top |
| `headline-icp-photo/` | Quiet Hours, Teyra | A solid brand colour with a big headline, then a smaller photo with two or three product chips overlapping its edge |

All brands, people, quotes, and logos are fictional. In a real ad, use the client's own quotes and logos, and the label from their own site.
