# Before and after (BA)

Two panels side by side: the buyer's world today, and the same job with the product. The contrast does the selling. Examples: `references/examples/before-after/`.

## Anatomy

1. **Headline**, centred at the top: the job ("A clearer way to sell.", "From handoff chaos to a clear next step.").
2. **Two panels**, equal width, rounded 24–32px, 24px apart. Labels at the top of each: "Before" / "After" or "Without <Product>" / "With <Product>".
3. **Before panel** is muted: grey or desaturated brand tint, crowded, many items.
4. **After panel** is the brand: accent fill or a light brand tint, calm, one or two items, or the product logo alone.
5. **Logo** bottom centre (Relay), or top right (Kairova).

## Variants (the variation axis)

- **list** (default): the before panel holds 4–6 chores as white cards, each with a ✕, slightly tilted and offset down the whole panel so it reads as a mess; the after panel holds one ✓ outcome, then a real product screenshot bleeding off its right edge. Kairova's crossed-out tasks with "No owner" are this pattern.
- **ui:** messy UI on the left (notifications, stacked windows, red error states), clean UI on the right (a tidy list, a green state). Kairova (a pile of reply-all emails against one handoff card) and Relay (sticky notes against a deal board). Build the UI as editable shapes, or use real screenshots.
- **metaphor:** one object each side, small and slow vs big and fast (paper plane vs airliner, bicycle vs motorbike, old car vs muscle car). The objects are images (generated or stock) or simple illustrations.

## Copy

- Headline names the job, not the product benefit.
- Before items: 4–7 real chores in the buyer's words, 1–3 words each.
- After: one outcome, or nothing but the product.
- Keep the labels short: "Before" / "After" beats sentences.

## Avoid

- Unequal panels, or an "after" that looks busier than the "before".
- Red and green as the only signal (colour-blind readers); add a cross and a tick.
