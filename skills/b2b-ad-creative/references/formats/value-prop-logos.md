# Value proposition with logos (VL)

A strong claim and the companies that back it up. Examples: `references/examples/value-prop-logos/`.

## Anatomy

1. **Logo** top right (Forma) or top left (Havelo).
2. **Headline**, left aligned, bold, 3 lines at most, with the payoff in the accent colour ("Bring every project **into focus**.").
3. **Subline**: one line.
4. **CTA pill** in the accent colour.
5. **Logo label and row**: the site's own label above the logos, word for word ("Trusted by teams at", "Used by teams at:"), then 4–8 real customer logos, one tone, same height. A site's single logo strip goes in as one row (`logoStrip: true`).
6. **Background**: never plain white: an off-white or light brand tint (Forma), or a solid brand colour (Havelo).

## Grid

| | 1080×1080 | 1080×1350 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Margin | 80 | 80 |
| Headline | top 150, 72–88px | top 190, 80–96px |
| Subline | under headline, 32px | 34px |
| CTA | under subline, height 72 | height 76 |
| Logo grid | bottom 96, 4 columns, cell 210×80, row gap 40 | bottom 120 |

## Variants

- **light:** off-white, dark text (default; Forma).
- **dark:** dark brand background, light logos (Havelo).
- **integrations:** instead of customer logos, the tools it connects to, as app icons on rounded tiles. Only real integrations.

## Copy

- Headline: who uses it or what it achieves, with a number if the client has one ("Trusted by 1,000+ finance teams").
- Subline: what they get.
- CTA: the offer.

## Logos

- Only from the client's own site or a list they gave. List each one in `to-confirm.md`.
- Recolour to one tone in Figma (SVGs make this easy). Never stretch a logo; fit it inside its cell.
