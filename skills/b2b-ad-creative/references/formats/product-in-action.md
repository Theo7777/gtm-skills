# Product in action (PA)

A big headline, then the product itself: a real screenshot with rounded corners, usually bleeding off the bottom or right edge so it feels like a window into the product. No browser frame needed. Examples: `references/examples/product-in-action/`.

## Anatomy

1. **Logo** top left (Metric) or top right (Nivaro).
2. **Headline**, left aligned, large (the outcome: "Give every customer a better answer."). A serif or the brand display font works well here.
3. **Optional CTA pill** under the headline ("Explore Metric", "See Nivaro").
4. **Screenshot**: rounded 20–28px, soft shadow, white card. Bleeds off the bottom, or the bottom and right.
5. **Background**: a flat pale brand tint (Metric citron, Nivaro sky blue) or a solid dark brand colour.

## Grid

| | 1080×1080 | 1080×1350 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Margin | 72 | 72 |
| Logo | top 64, height 48 | top 72, height 52 |
| Headline | top 170, 64–80px, up to 3 lines, width 900 | top 190, 72–88px |
| Screenshot | x 180, y 520, width 1000 (bleeds right), height to bottom | x 160, y 640, width 1000 |

## Variants

- **bleed:** screenshot bleeds off the bottom and right (default).
- **centred:** screenshot centred, fully visible, with a floating chip or two.
- **dark-quote:** dark brand background, a customer result as the headline in quotes, the product on a laptop or as a centred card, a review rating at the bottom. Use only with a real, sourced result.

## The screenshot

- Real product, cropped to the one screen that proves the headline. Crop tight: the reader needs one idea, not the whole app.
- If there is no real screenshot, build an illustrative UI from editable shapes (cards, rows, pills, a chart) using the brand colours, and flag it "illustrative".
- Blur or replace any real customer names, emails, or figures visible in the screenshot.

## Copy

- Headline: the outcome the screen delivers, 6–14 words.
- Optional subline: one line of how.

## Avoid

- Tiny screenshots that nobody can read at phone size.
- Laptop mockups as the default: they shrink the product. Use them only for the dark-quote variant.
