# Headline and ICP photo (IP)

A solid brand colour, a big result headline, and a smaller photo of the buyer's team with floating product chips over its corner. Examples: `references/examples/headline-icp-photo/`.

## Anatomy

1. **Background**: one solid brand colour (dark or deep), sometimes a lighter band at the very top behind the logo.
2. **Logo** top centre (or top left).
3. **Headline**, centred, 2–3 lines, white or light: a customer result ("<Customer> cut meeting time in half with <Product>") or an outcome with one accent phrase ("Make every launch feel **joined up**.").
4. **Photo**: rounded 24px top-left corner (or all corners), anchored bottom right, roughly 60% of the width and 40% of the height, often bleeding off the bottom and right.
5. **Product chips**: 1–3 white rounded cards overlapping the photo's left edge: a label chip (icon square + one word, "Marketing", "IT requests") and a mini card (rows, a progress bar, a donut). Built from editable shapes in the brand colours.
6. **Optional abstraction**: a soft shape, a line connector, or an accent blob relating to the product. Optional, small, never over a face.

## Variants

- **result:** a customer result headline (only with a sourced number; the photo is illustrative, so never imply the people are that customer's staff).
- **outcome:** an outcome headline with one accent phrase (Quiet Hours, Teyra).
- **light:** a bright or pale background with a dark headline.

## Image prompt

> Photorealistic, candid photo of [2–3 people matching the ICP: roles, mix of ages and backgrounds] [doing a real task together: reviewing a laptop, discussing a report] in [a modern, real-looking workplace for the industry]. Warm natural light, shallow depth of field, people in the right two thirds, looking at the work. Editorial, not stock-smiley. No text, no logos, no readable screens, no watermark.

Generated people are illustrative: a result headline must name the real customer only as text, never imply the photo shows them.
