# Copy rules

B2B buyers scroll LinkedIn between meetings. The ad has a second to say what changes for them, and a second more to make them believe it.

## Every format

- **Headline:** one idea, 4–10 words, the outcome or the pain in the buyer's words. Sentence case, or uppercase for the ICP background format only.
- **Accent words:** at most one phrase per headline in the accent colour, and it must be the payoff ("launch faster", "productivity increase by 20–30%"), never a filler word.
- **Specific beats clever.** "Reports done in minutes, not days" beats "Work smarter".
- **No jargon the buyer would not say.** Use their nouns (reports, payroll runs, renewals), not ours (solution, platform, leverage, streamline, seamless, robust).
- **British English, no em-dashes, no exclamation marks, no emojis** unless the brand's own ads use them.
- **CTA:** 2–4 words, a verb and what they get: "See the workflow", "Read the case study", "Try it free".

## Proof

- **Every number has a source:** the client's website, case study, or a figure the user gave. Note the URL or file in `copy.md`.
- **Testimonials are verbatim.** Trim with an ellipsis if needed; never reword. A specific outcome (a percentage, a time, money, a count) makes a stronger ad, so prefer those quotes, but any clear, attributed quote works.
- **Named people and customers** appear only if the client already shows them publicly (their site, case studies, or reviews). Record where.
- **Logos** come only from the client's site or a list they gave. A logo wall implies endorsement: list every logo in `to-confirm.md`.
- **Generated people are illustrative.** Never caption a generated photo as a named customer, and never pair a generated face with a real quote.

## Variation axes (pick one per pair of variations)

- **Message:** pain (the before) or outcome (the after).
- **Proof point:** a different result, customer, or feature.
- **Treatment:** light or dark background; brand colour or neutral.
- **Visual:** UI or metaphor; photo or illustration; logo or headshot.

## Character guide (LinkedIn and Meta statics)

| Field | Aim |
| --- | --- |
| Headline on the image | 4–10 words |
| Subline | Up to 15 words |
| Tags or pills | 1–3 words each, 2–4 of them |
| Quote | Up to 30 words |
| CTA | 2–4 words |

## LinkedIn and Meta

Reported (vendor sample, not verified): LinkedIn B2B statics usually carry the headline in the image, typography cards and screenshots are common; Meta B2B statics that run longest often have little or no text on the image, with people photography and the argument in the primary text. So for a Meta-heavy campaign, offer one low-text variation (IB or IP with the headline moved into the primary text). See `research.md`.

The platform text above and below the image (intro text, headline, description) is not part of this skill; use `ad-creative` for those fields.
