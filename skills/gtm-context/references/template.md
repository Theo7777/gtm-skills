## Section notes

- **Primary anchor:** use a category in mature markets, a use case where no category exists yet, and a competitive alternative when everyone knows and dislikes the old way.
- **Target audience:** a segment without a use case is too broad. "Finance teams" is weak. "Finance teams at 50 to 500 person companies closing the books in spreadsheets" is usable.
- **Competitive alternatives:** always include "do nothing". It is often the real competitor.
- **Proof points:** a stat or quote without a source goes under open questions and assumptions until confirmed.
- **Words to avoid:** include banned marketing clichés, competitor terms you do not want to echo, and internal jargon customers do not use.
