# Scoring rubric starter

Use this to propose a signal list when the context file does not define one. Adjust to the ICP and confirm with the user before scoring. Weights reflect how strongly a signal predicts a purchase in the next 90 days for a typical B2B product.

## Fit gate criteria

Write each criterion as a clear pass or fail test.

| Criterion | Example pass test |
|---|---|
| Company type | B2B SaaS or tech-enabled services |
| Size | 20 to 500 employees |
| Geography | UK or EU headquarters |
| Business model | Sells to businesses, not consumers |
| Buyer exists | Has a marketing or growth lead in post |
| Disqualifiers | Agency, competitor, or in administration |

An account fails the gate if it fails any must-have or hits any disqualifier. Mark it Unclear when the data is missing rather than guessing.

## Signal library

### High (10 points)
- Raised a funding round
- Hiring for the role that buys or uses the product
- New leader in the buying role (joined in the last 90 days)
- Publicly announced the problem the product solves (post, talk, or job ad wording)
- Visited the pricing page or requested a demo (first-party data only)

### Medium (5 points)
- Launched a new product, market, or pricing model
- Headcount growth above 20% in six months
- Using a complementary tool, or dropping a competitor's tool
- Engaged with the user's content or events (first-party data only)
- Expanded into a new country

### Low (2 points)
- General hiring activity unrelated to the buying role
- Industry award or press mention
- Leadership posting about related topics
- Attended a relevant industry event

## Worked example (fictional)

Northwind Analytics, 80 staff, London SaaS. Fit.
- Hiring a Head of Growth, 12 days ago (High, 10)
- Launched a self-serve plan, 40 days ago (Medium, 5)
- CEO podcast appearance on scaling, 60 days ago (Low, 2)

Total 17 points, so band B. Warm. Action: targeted campaign plus a personal note to the CEO referencing the self-serve launch.

## Where to find signals

- Funding: company press page, Crunchbase, news search
- Hiring: company careers page, LinkedIn Jobs
- Leadership changes: LinkedIn company page, press releases
- Product launches: company blog, changelog, Product Hunt
- Headcount: LinkedIn company page employee count over time
