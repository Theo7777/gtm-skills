# Signal library

41 signals that make outreach timely, grouped by where you would spot them. Channel and funnel stage are rough planning guides.

Inspired by Common Room's signals library (https://www.commonroom.io/resources/signals/).

Each signal is a trigger that makes outreach timely. Strength tells you how much weight the email can put on it: a high signal can carry the whole hook, a low signal needs a second observation alongside it.

If none fit, use a **custom signal**: the user describes the trigger in their own words and, if they have one, a screenshot or link.

## Sales-led (28)

### Champion Changed Jobs
`champion-changed-jobs` · strength: **high** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

A past customer or power user just moved to a new company. They already know and trust your product, making them warm leads at their new org. Reach out to congratulate them and offer to help them succeed in their new role.

### Uses Relevant Tech Stack
`uses-relevant-tech-stack` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

The prospect uses tools that integrate with or compete with yours. Reference their stack to show you understand their workflow, speak their language, and can add immediate value.

Ask for context: Which specific tools? e.g., Trello, HubSpot, Salesforce...
Screenshot hint: Screenshot from BuiltWith, Wappalyzer, or similar

### Reviving Past Conversation
`reviving-past-conversation` · strength: **high** · channel: CRM · funnel: Mid: Consideration

A deal went cold due to timing, budget, or missing features. Circle back with a fresh angle. Maybe you've shipped what they needed, or their situation has changed. These are often your lowest-hanging fruit.

### Affected by New Regulation
`affected-by-regulation` · strength: **high** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Mid: Consideration

New legislation or compliance requirements impact their business. If your product helps them comply or adapt, you have a compelling and timely reason to reach out.

Ask for context: Which regulation? e.g., GDPR, SOC2, new industry law...
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of news article or regulation details

### Impacted by Platform Policy Change
`impacted-by-platform-change` · strength: **high** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Mid: Consideration

A platform they rely on (Google, Apple, Meta, etc.) changed its policies, causing disruption. Your product might solve the problem they now face. Reference the specific change in your outreach.

Ask for context: Which platform and what changed? e.g., Apple ATT, Google cookie deprecation...
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of the announcement or news coverage

### Competitor Deprecated a Feature
`competitor-deprecated-feature` · strength: **high** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Mid: Consideration

A competitor stopped supporting a feature their customers relied on. Those users are now looking for alternatives. Position yourself as the solution to their newly created problem.

Ask for context: Which competitor and what feature? e.g., which feature, and when it stops working...
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of deprecation announcement

### Competitor Shut Down or Acquired
`competitor-shut-down` · strength: **high** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Mid: Consideration

A competitor closed or got acquired, leaving their customers stranded. These prospects are actively seeking alternatives. Move fast with a migration-focused message.

Ask for context: Which competitor? Any details about the shutdown/acquisition?
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of news or announcement

### Company Had Layoffs
`company-had-layoffs` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

Recent layoffs signal a need for efficiency or strategy shift. Your product might help them do more with less, consolidate tools, or fill gaps left by departures.

### Removed Tool from Tech Stack
`removed-tool-from-stack` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Mid: Consideration

They recently dropped a tool from their stack. They might be consolidating, cutting costs, or unhappy with the solution. Position yourself as the better alternative.

Ask for context: Which tool did they remove? e.g., Intercom, Drift...
Screenshot hint: Screenshot showing the change

### Left Negative Review of Competitor
`left-negative-competitor-review` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They left a critical review on G2, Capterra, or similar. You know exactly what pain points they have. Address those specific frustrations in your outreach.

Ask for context: Key points from their review? What were they frustrated about?
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of the review from G2, Capterra, etc.

### Ex-Competitor Employee
`ex-competitor-employee` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

Someone who used to work at a competitor just started a new role. They have insider knowledge and might be open to trying a different solution. A great source for intel and warm conversations.

### Company in Negative Press
`company-in-negative-press` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They're dealing with PR issues or public criticism. If your product can help address the underlying problem, you have a timely and relevant reason to reach out. Tread carefully and be helpful, not opportunistic.

### Company is Hiring Heavily
`company-hiring-heavily` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

Rapid hiring signals growth and new initiatives. New teams need new tools. Position your product as essential infrastructure for their scaling efforts.

### Target Buyer Just Started
`target-buyer-just-started` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

Your ideal buyer, or a new executive such as a CEO, CRO, or CMO, just joined a target company. New leaders want to make an impact quickly and often move budget to new priorities. Help them build their ideal stack and hit early wins.

### Engaged with LinkedIn Post
`engaged-with-linkedin-post` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They liked, commented on, or shared a relevant LinkedIn post (yours, industry-related, or a trending post about a problem you solve). This shows active interest in the topic. Reference the post to start a warm conversation.

Ask for context: What was the post about? What did they comment?
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of the LinkedIn post or their comment

### Downloaded Your Content
`downloaded-content` · strength: **medium** · channel: Website · funnel: Top: Awareness

They downloaded your ebook, whitepaper, or gated content. They're actively researching the problem you solve. Follow up with additional value related to what they downloaded.

### Attended Your Webinar
`attended-webinar` · strength: **medium** · channel: Website · funnel: Top: Awareness

They attended or registered for your webinar. This signals active interest and desire to learn more. Follow up with key takeaways and an offer to dive deeper.

### Recently Raised Funding
`recently-raised-funding` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They just announced a funding round. Growth is coming and they'll need tools to scale. But don't lead with 'congrats on the funding', because everyone does that. Focus on how you can help with their next chapter.

Ask for context: Round size, lead investor, and what they say the money is for.
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of TechCrunch, Crunchbase, or announcement

### Published Financial Report
`published-financial-report` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

Their earnings call or 10-K revealed strategic priorities. Reference specific initiatives they mentioned to show you've done your homework and can help with their stated goals.

### Relaunched Website
`relaunched-website` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

A major website overhaul can signal a strategic shift: new positioning, new audience, or going upmarket. Your product might align with their new direction.

### Featured on Podcast
`featured-on-podcast` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

Your target buyer appeared on a podcast. Listen to it, reference something specific they said, and show genuine interest in their perspective. Great way to build rapport.

Ask for context: Which podcast? What did they discuss? Any key quotes?
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of the podcast episode or show notes

### Posted About Relevant Topic
`posted-about-relevant-topic` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They published a post about a topic your product addresses. Engage thoughtfully with their content first, then follow up with value-add insights or resources.

Ask for context: What was the post about? Key points or quotes?
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of their LinkedIn/Twitter post

### Launched New Product or Feature
`launched-new-product` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They just shipped something new. This signals growth, possibly new customer segments, and new operational needs. Congratulate them and connect your product to their new direction.

Ask for context: What did they launch? Key details about the product/feature?
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of Product Hunt, launch tweet, or announcement

### Announced Merger or Acquisition
`announced-merger-acquisition` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

Mergers create disruption: system consolidation, new processes, culture clashes. Your product might help smooth the transition or solve newly created problems.

### Posted Relevant Job Opening
`posted-relevant-job` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They're hiring for a role your product supports (e.g., hiring SDRs when you sell sales tools). The job post reveals their priorities and pain points. Reference specific requirements from the listing.

Ask for context: What role? Key requirements from the job post?
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of the job posting

### Won Award or Recognition
`won-award` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They made a list (Cloud 100, Inc 5000, etc.) or won an industry award. Congratulate them genuinely, then pivot to how you can help them maintain momentum.

### Got Promoted
`got-promoted` · strength: **medium** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

Your contact got promoted. They likely have expanded scope, new budget, and pressure to deliver results. Congratulate them and offer to help them succeed in their new role.

### Expanding to New Region
`expanding-to-new-region` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They're entering a new market or geography. This creates needs around localisation, compliance, hiring, and operations. Position your product as essential for their expansion.

Ask for context: Which region are they expanding to? Any details about their plans?
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of expansion announcement

## Competitor (2)

### Competitor Renewal Coming Up
`competitor-renewal-coming` · strength: **high** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Mid: Consideration

Intel suggests they're approaching renewal with a competitor. Renewals are decision points. Present yourself as the alternative worth evaluating.

### Mentioned or Followed Competitor
`mentioned-competitor` · strength: **medium** · channel: Community · funnel: Top: Awareness

They mentioned a competitor in a post, comment, or community, or just followed one on LinkedIn. They're thinking about the space. Join the conversation or follow up with your perspective.

## Community-led (4)

### Active on Relevant GitHub Repo
`active-on-github` · strength: **medium** · channel: Open Source · funnel: Mid: Consideration

They starred, forked, or contributed to a relevant open-source project. This signals technical interest and active problem-solving. Reference their GitHub activity in your outreach.

### Decision Maker Engaged
`decision-maker-engaged` · strength: **high** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

An economic buyer (VP, Director, C-level) showed themselves. They joined your community, engaged with content, or attended an event. These are rare and valuable. Prioritise them.

### Joined Your Community
`joined-your-community` · strength: **medium** · channel: Community · funnel: Mid: Consideration

They joined your Slack, Discord, or community forum. They're seeking knowledge and peer support. Welcome them warmly and offer to help with whatever brought them there.

### Asked Question in Community
`asked-question-in-community` · strength: **high** · channel: Community · funnel: Mid: Consideration

They posted a question in a community or forum. They need help right now. Provide genuine value first, then follow up with how your product can help.

Ask for context: What question did they ask? Which community/forum?
Screenshot hint: Screenshot of their question/post

## Event (3)

### Attended Industry Event
`attended-industry-event` · strength: **low** · channel: Community · funnel: Top: Awareness

They attended or registered for a conference, event, or industry webinar (even if you weren't there). This signals they're investing time in learning. Reference the event and offer relevant follow-up insights. If you'll be there too, reach out beforehand to arrange a conversation during the event.

### Speaking at Event
`speaking-at-event` · strength: **medium** · channel: Community · funnel: Top: Awareness

They're speaking at an upcoming conference or panel. This reveals their expertise and interests. Attend their talk, engage with their ideas, and follow up afterward.

### Sponsoring Event
`sponsoring-event` · strength: **low** · channel: Dark Funnel · funnel: Top: Awareness

They're sponsoring a conference. They want to reach that audience. If your product helps with their sponsorship goals (lead gen, brand awareness), you have a relevant angle.

## Nearbound (4)

### Their Company Uses Your Product
`uses-your-product-internally` · strength: **high** · channel: CRM · funnel: Mid: Consideration

You're already a vendor to their company. Use this existing relationship as leverage. You've proven value, and there may be expansion opportunities in other departments.

### Shared Deal with Partner
`shared-deal-with-partner` · strength: **medium** · channel: CRM · funnel: Mid: Consideration

A strategic partner is also working this account. Coordinate with your partner for a warm intro or co-selling approach. Two voices are stronger than one.

### Shared Investor or Adviser
`shared-investor` · strength: **medium** · channel: Community · funnel: Top: Awareness

You share an investor or adviser with the target company. Ask them for a warm intro, with specific context on why it's relevant. Investors have a vested interest in both companies succeeding.

### Mutual Connection
`mutual-connection` · strength: **medium** · channel: Community · funnel: Top: Awareness

You have a shared connection (colleague, friend, former coworker) with the prospect. Ask for a warm intro or permission to name-drop. Personal connections cut through the noise.
