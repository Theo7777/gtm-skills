# Objection crusher patterns

Most objections fall into one of six types. Find the type, then pick a crusher pattern. All examples use fictional products.

| Objection type | What the buyer thinks | Crusher patterns | Example headline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost | "Too expensive" or "I'll get stung later" | "Start with just [price]." "Free for [period]." "No hidden fees." "No card required." | Track every invoice automatically. Free for small teams. |
| Effort | "This will take ages to set up" | "Set up in [X] minutes." "No setup needed." "Works with your current tools." | Book more calls on autopilot. Set up in five minutes. |
| Skill | "I'm not technical enough" | "No coding needed." "No design skills required." "Built for beginners." | Launch your online shop today. No coding needed. |
| Risk | "What if it doesn't work?" | "Cancel anytime." "Money-back guarantee." "Try it free." | Get fitter at home. Cancel anytime. |
| Time | "I'll wait too long for results" | "Results in [X] days." "Delivered in [X] minutes." "Live today." | Get your logo designed. Delivered in 48 hours. |
| Switching | "Moving everything over is painful" | "Import in one click." "Keep your current number." "We move your data for you." | Switch your team's CRM. We migrate everything free. |

## Rules for crushers

- Only use a crusher the product can actually deliver. A false "cancel anytime" is worse than no crusher.
- If the claim needs a number (price, minutes, days) and you do not have one, write a placeholder like `[X] minutes` and flag it.
- One objection per headline. Two crushers in one line read as a list and lose the pause.

## Benefit openers that work

Start the first half with a verb the buyer wants to do: Get, Launch, Send, Book, Build, Start, Grow, Save, Find, Hire, Track, Ship.

Weak openers to replace:
- "Helps you..." becomes the verb itself.
- "The best way to..." becomes the action.
- "Unlock..." or "Supercharge..." becomes the concrete outcome.
